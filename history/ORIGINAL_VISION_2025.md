# 🚩 RAKSHAK-AI

### AI-powered technology for safety, security & protection.

> **From recording incidents to preventing them.**

## 🛡️ What is Rakshak-AI?

Rakshak-AI is an India-born AI initiative exploring how
Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision can be used to
build intelligent systems for safety, security and
real-time protection.

The vision is to move beyond security systems that simply
record incidents — toward systems that can understand
situations and assist when intervention is needed.

## 🎯 Our Vision

To build intelligent protective systems that can:

- 👁️ Observe real-world situations
- 🧠 Understand potentially dangerous situations
- 🚨 Trigger timely alerts
- 🤝 Assist human responders
- 🤖 Explore autonomous assistance

## 🔬 Areas of Exploration

- Artificial Intelligence
- Computer Vision
- Intelligent Surveillance
- Real-Time Safety Systems
- Emergency Response
- Autonomous Systems
- Privacy-Conscious Technology

## 🚧 Current Phase

**Private R&D & Prototyping**

Rakshak-AI is currently being developed and researched.

The public profile intentionally shares the project's
vision and direction while detailed architecture,
algorithms and implementation remain private during
development.

## 🇮🇳 The Vision

Technology should not only observe what happens.

It should help us respond when it matters.

> **Rakshak-AI — intelligence designed to protect.**
